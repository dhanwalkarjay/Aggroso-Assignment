Greenblock Neighbors is a registered nonprofit serving the East River neighborhood. Maya Chen, Program Director, is the primary contact at 18 Harbor Street. Residents helped shape this proposal during two listening sessions, where they asked for practical ways to reduce summer heat in older apartment buildings.

Our project will provide home energy workshops, distribute window weatherization kits, and train resident volunteers to lead follow-up visits. Maya Chen will coordinate the work, with support from volunteer building captains. We will begin in October, hold workshops monthly, and complete follow-up visits by June. We expect strong participation and will keep an attendance sheet for each workshop.

The project will help families understand energy-saving choices and prepare for extreme heat. We will track workshop attendance, kits distributed, and follow-up conversations. The team served 10,000 families last year through neighborhood programs, and this experience gives us a strong foundation for the proposed work.

We request $24,000. Personnel will total $9,000, materials $8,000, contracted workshop facilitators $5,000, and travel $2,000. Materials will cover weatherization kits and printed learning materials. Facilitator costs support the monthly workshops. The budget is connected to the activities described above.

We will share lessons at a neighborhood meeting and provide a short reflection after the project. The signed organizational authorization letter and a detailed schedule are not included in this draft. We have not yet finalized how we will measure household energy savings or identify the person authorized to accept grant funds.
